Privacy Policy: Cooking Tanuki: Japan Food Cafe

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Summary

Cooking Tanuki: Japan Food Cafe ("the game") is a casual cooking game. You do not need an account to play. We do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information, and we do not run a server that stores your data.

The game saves your progress on your own device. The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, and uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Crashlytics to learn how the game is played and to fix crashes. These collect some technical information. This policy explains all of this.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (coins, stars, upgrades, decorations, customer cards, the number of shifts you have left) and your settings (the volume of the music and of the sound effects) in the storage of your device.

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner ads and rewarded ads (ads you can choose to watch to get an extra shift). These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, shown or clicked, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (for example model, operating system version, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads (shown, clicked, watched);
  • diagnostic and performance data.

Google uses this information to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you allow it, to personalise ads. We do not receive this personal information. We only see summary reports (for example how many ads were shown).

More about how Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

To understand how the game is played, for example how many shifts are played, which cities and dishes are used, and where players stop, so that we can improve the game and fix problems, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google. It may collect:

  • an app instance identifier (a random number created for each installation; it is not your name or email address);
  • events about how you use the game (for example a shift started or finished, a city opened);
  • device and app information (for example model, operating system version, language, app version);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • where the law and your settings allow it, your advertising identifier.

We do not use accounts, so this data is not linked to who you are. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics follows your choice in the consent form: if you do not agree, no identifiers are stored on your device and only limited signals that do not identify you may be sent. More about how Google handles this data: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

The game also uses Firebase Crashlytics. If the game crashes or hits an error, a crash report is sent with technical details: the error, where in the program it happened, the device model, the operating system and app version, and a random installation identifier. It contains no personal information from you and is used only to find and fix bugs. Like analytics, crash reports are sent only after the consent check at the start of the game.

4. Your choices

  • Consent (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland). When you first open the game you may see a consent form from Google, which covers both ads and analytics. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings (the gear button on the home screen), then Privacy choices.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ads personalisation in your device settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads).
  • iOS. You can control tracking in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking, and limit ad personalisation in your device settings.
  • Not watching ads. Rewarded ads are optional. You can always choose not to watch them.

If you choose not to allow personalised ads, you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share information about you with anyone except as described in sections 2 and 3, where Google and its partners receive the data needed to show ads and to measure how the game is used.

6. Children

The game is meant for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and think a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will help.

7. Storage and security

We do not store your personal information on our own servers. Information that Google collects (through AdMob and Firebase) is kept and protected according to Google's own policies. Google may process information in countries other than the one you live in.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is usually nothing for us to give, correct or delete. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to ask Google about the data it holds, to withdraw your consent at any time, and to complain to a data protection authority. You can withdraw consent in the game as described in section 4. You can also contact us with any question.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game adds new features. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If a change is important, we will tell you in the game or in the store listing.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the game's listing on the App Store or Google Play.
